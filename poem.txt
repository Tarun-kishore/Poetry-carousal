TITLE: A Trial

A trial.

For a murder.

A man.

The accused.

---

“So, Mr. John Doe,

tell us—

when did you kill him?”

A question from the court.

---

“Four years ago.

His name was Cain.”

The defendant replied.

---

“Why did you kill him?”

Another question.

---

“I had to kill him

to continue living

in this cursed world.

He used to be a happy guy.

Trusted others.

Had empathy.

Had feelings.

Everything this world punishes.

---

Maybe he was

too good for this world.”

---

“But that doesn't give you

the right to kill him.”

---

“I had every right.

He used to give me hope—

that everything

would be fine.

He was an illusion of light

in this completely dark world.”

---

The judge listened

in silence.

Then lowered the head

in grief.

---

“Has the jury

reached a verdict?”

---

Twelve faces.

Familiar.

Yet somehow strangers.

They looked at the accused.

Then, in unison—

---

**“We find the defendant, John Doe,

also known as Cain

by his loved ones,

guilty of murder.”**

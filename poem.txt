# The Shadow

Ever since I can remember,

I knew one thing.

---

Even if I had no one with me,

I'd always have my shadow.

---

It went where I went.

Did what I did.

Whenever I looked back,

it was there.

---

With it by my side,

I never felt alone.

---

I had people come and go
in my life,

but there was always
one constant.

---

My shadow.

---

Time passed.

Years ran by.

Life grew harder.

---

The light in my life
began to fade.

I became surrounded
by darkness.

---

But I wasn't afraid.

I knew my companion
would still be there.

---

My shadow.

---

I looked around.

For my shadow.

---

But all I could see

was darkness.

---

Only darkness.

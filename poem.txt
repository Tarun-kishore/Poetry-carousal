TITLE: The Swordsman

There once was a swordsman
Who was called the greatest

Won every match
No one even came close

---

He had a scary scar
Along his left eye

---

One day an old man asked
"What's that scar?"

---

"It's from a sword."

---

"Who could've been skilled enough
to give you that?"

---

The swordsman smiled.

---

"Me."
